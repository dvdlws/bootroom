How It Works

Three Simple Steps

Step 1
Donate
Got cleats sitting in a closet? Tell us what you have and we'll arrange a drop-off. Any size.
Step 2
Ask
A parent or guardian tells us the child's shoe size. That's it.
Step 3
Play
We match a pair to the size. When a child outgrows them, they come back for another kid.
